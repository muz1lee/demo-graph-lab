# deposit_coin gold rationale (claude-bringup, 2026-07-29)

Run: `harness_deposit_coin_20260729_234831`. Frames cited as `tSSSS_ff` (stage folder timestamps) and `fNNN` (graph.json evidence indices, 25 fps). All contentious calls verified on 4–6x zoom crops — the coin is ~8 px in the raw 640px frames, so nothing here was judged from full frames alone.

Scene: one gold coin propped on a small **transparent stand** at table right; one pink piggy bank at table left, slit on its back running along the spine; right arm picks, left arm inserts, handover in the middle. graph.json covers stages 0–3; the trace's stage 4 (cleanup) is unextracted and unjudged.

## s0 — pick (0.0–2.0s)

The demo answers "how do you grasp a flat coin" by **not having a flat coin**: t0000_00 zoom shows the coin propped tilted on a transparent cradle, so a direct pinch is feasible and no slide-to-table-edge maneuver occurs. t0001_00→t0001_50 (f25/f38): wrist above, fingers descending — `approach_direction(top_down)` correct, and here it is load-bearing (a lateral sweep plows the stand/table), not a symmetry freebie; the free DoF is only the azimuth about the coin's face axis. t0001_50/t0002_00 (f38/f50): pinch lands on the exposed upper rim, coin lifted, stand left behind — `region_grasp(coin, top)` correct (constraint and acceptance), with the note that "rim" (the token s2 uses) would be sharper vocabulary. `center_align(gripper.center, coin.center)` demoted to **incidental**: the post-grasp view t0002_75 shows the coin protruding nearly a full diameter from the fingertips, i.e. the realized closing line is at the rim, not the center; coarse footprint centering is already entailed by region_grasp + approach, so exact center==center is one sample of an equivalence class. No missing items — deliberately not padded.

## s1 — handover (2.0–5.0s)

t0002_75 (f69): right gripper carries the coin mid-air (`carry(right_gripper_holds_coin)` correct). t0004_25 (f106): the receiving left gripper closes in **horizontally, anti-parallel** to the right, fingertip-to-fingertip — `approach_direction(side, coin)` correct; this is dictated by the giver's pose (a top-down receive collides with the right gripper's tall fingertip triangles bracketing the coin), so the rubric's symmetric-azimuth discount does not apply. `center_align(left_gripper.grasp_point, coin.center)` correct — and deliberately judged OPPOSITE to s0's center_align: here the graspable band is the narrow exposed strip between the right fingertips and the far rim, so aiming at the coin center is functionally pinned (too far out = miss, too far in = finger clash). `clearance(left_gripper, right_gripper)` correct — tip-to-tip convergence with bodies apart is what makes a dual-arm handover survivable. Acceptance `carry(left_gripper_holds_coin)` correct: t0005_00 (f125) shows the coin in the left fingertips with the right jaws sprung wide open. **Missing: `order(left_gripper_grasp_coin → right_gripper_release)`** — t0004_25 (both engaged) → t0005_00 (left holds, right open) proves the closure-before-release sequencing; reversed order drops the coin, and no sequencing constraint exists anywhere in the graph for this stage.

## s2 — fine_alignment (5.0–5.75s)

f125–f134 (t0005_00→t0005_38): rim hold with the far edge free — `region_grasp(coin, rim)` correct and doing double duty as the stage's in-hand/carry relation (why I did NOT add a missing `carry` here). The four goal-side items (`center_align`, `above`, `approach_direction(top_down, slot)`, plus both acceptance entries) are all judged **correct with a boundary-lag caveat**: at the cited f139/f144 (t0005_56/t0005_75) the coin is demonstrably still up-right of the slit — over the pig's head/ear region — and truly-above-the-slot is first visible at stage03/t0005_94, ~0.2 s after the 5.75 s cut. The relations are the right core content for an alignment stage and the demo does establish them; the segmenter's boundary is simply early relative to the settled state. Gold consumers should read these six verdicts as "correct, loose temporal tolerance", the mirror image of insert_tubes' s0/s2 mis-staging (there the settled state was imported backwards and judged wrong; here the claim is the stage's own goal predicate, reached at boundary+ε, so it stays correct). `approach_direction(top_down, coin_bank.slot)` is fixed by the upward-opening slit — same reasoning as the insert_tubes hole axis — correct.

**Missing: `axis_parallel(coin.plane_normal ∥ coin_bank.slot.plane_normal)` — the headline defect of this extraction.** The coin must enter a thin slit edge-first, so its plane has to be brought parallel to the slit plane before descent; t0005_94/t0006_12 show the coin edge-on, elongated along the slit direction at the mouth. The extractor visibly *knew* the geometry — this very stage carves `coin_normal_axis` and `slot_axis` holes (3/5 each) — but never bound them into a constraint. So this is a **binding failure, not a vocabulary gap**: `axis_parallel` exists in the vocab and symbolic axis args would have fit. Symmetry cuts both ways here, exactly per the task design: the spin about the coin's own normal is a free DoF (correctly unconstrained), while the two plane-orientation DoF are core and went missing.

## s3 — insertion (5.75–6.5s)

t0005_94→t0006_31 (f144–f158): vertical descent onto the slit, coin over the mouth, entering, mostly swallowed; t0006_50 (f162): coin gone from view, fingertip pads resting at the back surface. `approach_direction(top_down)`, `above`, `center_align`, `inside` all correct — `inside` is the task goal and the acceptance `inside` is the correct, sufficient success check (empty slit + empty retracting gripper confirmed at stage04/t0007_68). Acceptance `center_align` demoted to **incidental**: true en route (f153/f158) but not a postcondition — once the coin passes through it is unobservable (invisible at f162) and success is fully captured by `inside`; a during-constraint was duplicated into acceptance. Contrast with s2, where acceptance center_align IS the stage's product and stays correct.

**Missing (3):** (1) `axis_parallel` again — plane parallelism must be *maintained* through descent, any twist wedges the disc at the mouth; same unbound-holes evidence as s2. (2) `clearance(left_gripper, coin_bank)` — the bank is free-standing (right arm retracted since t0005_38, nothing braces the pig); the demo stops the pads at the back surface while the coin drops through, and the `insertion_depth` hole (5/5!) shows the extractor sensed the depth limit without emitting any constraint bounding it. The pig's pose looks slightly disturbed by stage04/t0007_68 — the margin was real and thin. (3) `order(coin_inside_slot → gripper_release)` — the coin is gripper-guided into the slit (t0006_12/t0006_31) and freed only once inside (t0006_50); early release drops it flat on the pig's back. stages.json even assigns `eef_event: release` to cleanup — the ordering exists in the trace and vanished from the graph.

## Cross-stage notes

- **Ambiguity check (deposit_coin is our AMBIGUITY-class task): this demo contains NO choice ambiguity.** Every frame shows exactly one coin, one bank, one slit (t0000_00, t0002_75, t0005_56 verified on zoom); the only other small object is the transparent grasp-stand, a support fixture a weak detector could conceivably mistake for a second coin, but nothing requiring a which-coin/which-slot decision. The extraction was therefore never tested on the class's defining challenge — a gold for the ambiguity axis needs a multi-coin or multi-slot recording; this run only certifies the geometry axis.
- **The graph is precision-strong, recall-weak**: 0 wrong out of 21 judged items (P = 19/19 on the P/R denominators) but 5 missing against 19 correct (R = 19/24 ≈ 0.79), and the misses cluster on exactly the two families the coin-in-slit task exists to probe: plane/axis alignment (axis_parallel ×2) and sequencing/interaction constraints (order ×2, clearance ×1).
- **Holes-vs-constraints split personality**: three times the extractor carved the right numeric holes (coin_normal_axis + slot_axis at s2, insertion_depth 5/5 at s3) and three times failed to emit the symbolic constraint those holes exist to serve. Worth tracking as its own failure mode in metrics — it is not perception failure, it is graph-assembly failure.
- **Grasp transfer warning**: every grasp/approach verdict at s0 is conditioned on the propped-on-stand pose. On a genuinely flat coin, top_down + top-rim pinch does not transfer (a slide-to-edge or push-against-fixture maneuver would be needed); the demo sidesteps the classic thin-object grasp problem rather than teaching it.
- The two `center_align` demotions (s0 constraint, s3 acceptance) plus the two upgrades of nominally similar items elsewhere (s1 center_align, s2 acceptance center_align) are deliberate function-over-name judgments: identical vocab items flip between core and incidental depending on which DoF the stage actually pins.
